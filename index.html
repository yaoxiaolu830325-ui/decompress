Decompress

Three tiny toys for your hands. No goals, no timers, no ads. Open one, poke at it for a minute, close the tab.

Bubble Wrap

Infinite bubble wrap. Drag across the sheet — every bubble dents and stays dented.

Silk

Draw ribbons of light and let go. They flow, sway and slowly fall away.

Sand

Pour colored sand with a finger. It piles up, slides and settles where you leave it.

Snake

The classic, rebuilt smooth: neon body, pulsing food, speed that creeps up on you.
